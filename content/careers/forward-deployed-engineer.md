You will spend time on site with estimators, planners and plant managers, map how their work actually flows, and deploy Aerium's Foundation, Forge models and Workforce agents into their day. You own the outcome from first visit to production.

## What you will do

- Spend time on site with customers to learn how quoting, costing and supply chain really run.
- Connect their systems into Foundation, including ERPs, CAD, inboxes and machines with no API.
- Build and tune agents that take real work off people's plates, then measure the result.
- Bring what you learn back into the product so the next deployment is faster.

## What we look for

- Strong software engineering, ideally Python and TypeScript, shipped to production.
- Comfort with messy data and systems you did not design.
- You enjoy sitting with customers and can explain technical trade-offs in plain language.
- Willing to travel to customer sites across North America.

## Nice to have

- Experience in manufacturing, industrial or operations software.
- Hands-on work with LLMs, agents or retrieval in production.
- Familiarity with ERPs like Dynamics 365, or CAD tools like SolidWorks.
